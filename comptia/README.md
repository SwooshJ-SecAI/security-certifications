# CompTIA Security+ (SY0-701)

Study notes and reference material for the CompTIA Security+ SY0-701 exam. Security+ is the foundational security certification — it establishes the vocabulary and mental models that later credentials assume.

---

## Exam Structure

The SY0-701 exam is organized into five domains, each weighted by its share of exam questions:

| # | Domain | Weight |
|:--|:---|:--:|
| 1 | General Security Concepts | 12% |
| 2 | Threats, Vulnerabilities, and Mitigations | 22% |
| 3 | Security Architecture | 18% |
| 4 | Security Operations | 28% |
| 5 | Security Program Management and Oversight | 20% |

Domain 4 (Security Operations) carries the most weight — the exam is operations-heavy, favoring the practitioner who understands how controls run day to day over the candidate who only memorizes definitions.

## Study Approach

1. **Learn the vocabulary first.** Domain 1 is small (12%) but disproportionately important — every other domain reuses its terms. Do not move on until the CIA triad, the control types, and the cryptographic primitives are second nature.
2. **Anchor threats to mitigations.** Domain 2 and Domain 4 are two sides of one coin: for every threat, know the control that answers it. Study them as pairs, not separate lists.
3. **Reason about architecture, do not memorize diagrams.** Domain 3 rewards understanding why a design places a control where it does — segmentation, zero trust, resilience — over recalling a specific topology.
4. **Practice with scenario questions.** The exam includes performance-based questions. Reading is not enough; the material has to be applied to a described situation.

The goal is understanding that transfers to the job, with the passing score as the checkpoint.
